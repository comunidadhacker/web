About Crea Planetas Get it on Google Play

Comunidad Hacker Crea Juegos makes games for learning by playing.

Not "educational" games with a test hidden behind them. Toys: things you can move to see what happens, built on real models, so that what happens is what would happen.

HOW WE MAKE THEM

  • Nothing is drawn in advance. Everything you see is computed from the model, so no two worlds are alike and none of them is an illustration of a world.
  • The model is real and checkable. The equations are the published ones, with their authors, and the results are compared against measured things: Earth, Mars, Venus.
  • If the model answers something inconvenient, we keep it. A game that lies to look pretty teaches the pretty thing and the false thing together.
  • You can play without reading. Every quantity appears as a picture, as a bar and as a number, in that order of importance, because a four-year-old is entitled to play with physics.
  • It works offline and without an account. A phone should not have to ask anyone's permission for a game to run.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

  • No ads.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No accounts, no sign-up.
  • No data collection: no analytics, no identifiers, no location.
  • No system permissions requested.

Our games are free, and free for real: there is nothing inside being sold, neither your attention nor your data.
